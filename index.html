HOW TO READ THESE RESULTS

Search methods & limitations

  • Gene names are normalized with HGNC lookup when available; aliases are included in literature queries. Cancer wording is passed to source APIs and may not capture every synonym.
  • Literature searches combine PubMed and Europe PMC. Results are deduplicated by PMID/DOI where identifiers exist. This is not an exhaustive systematic review; query syntax, indexing, and upstream availability affect recall.
  • Relevance sorting is a search aid, not an evidence-strength score. Filters use source metadata and can miss records with incomplete indexing.
  • Therapy examples are limited, curated starter associations. Verify exact indication and local status with the linked regulator and clinical guideline. No status is inferred from publication count.
  • Clinical trial status is copied from registry records and may change; contact sites to confirm recruitment. Completed registration does not equal positive results.
  • Sources may fail or rate-limit. Each result panel shows which sources returned data and when the search ran.
  • No patient-level data should be entered. Searches are stored in local browser storage.
